Terms & Privacy Policy

This page explains how EcoSprout collects, uses and protects customer information and the terms that apply when using our website.

1. Privacy Policy

EcoSprout respects the privacy of its customers and website visitors. We are committed to handling personal information responsibly and securely. By using the EcoSprout website, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information when you register an account, place an order, request a gardening service, register for an event, or submit an inquiry.

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Home or delivery address
  • Account login information
  • Order and service request details
  • Workshop and event registration details
  • Messages and customer inquiries

3. How We Use Your Information

The information collected by EcoSprout may be used to provide and improve our services, process customer orders and communicate with customers.

  • Creating and managing customer accounts
  • Processing plant and gardening tool orders
  • Managing service requests and bookings
  • Managing workshop and event registrations
  • Responding to customer inquiries
  • Improving our website and services
  • Maintaining system security

4. Payments

Customers may be required to provide payment information when purchasing products or services. EcoSprout aims to handle payment transactions securely. Payment information should only be used for completing authorized transactions.

5. Cookies and Website Usage

EcoSprout may use cookies or similar technologies to improve the browsing experience, maintain login sessions and understand how visitors use the website. Users may control cookie settings through their browser.

6. Data Security

EcoSprout takes reasonable steps to protect customer information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse or modification. Users are responsible for keeping their account passwords confidential and should not share login information with other people.

7. Customer Accounts

Customers are responsible for providing accurate information when creating an EcoSprout account. Users must keep their account information up to date and are responsible for activities performed through their account.

8. Orders and Product Availability

Plant and gardening product availability may change depending on inventory levels. EcoSprout may update product information, prices and availability when necessary.

9. Gardening Services

Customers may request gardening and landscaping services through the EcoSprout website. Service availability, scheduling and pricing may depend on the requested service and customer location.

10. Events and Workshops

EcoSprout may provide educational workshops and gardening events. Customers are responsible for providing correct registration information. Event schedules and availability may change when necessary.

11. Acceptable Use

Users must not misuse the EcoSprout website, attempt unauthorized access to system data, interfere with website functionality or use the platform for unlawful purposes.

12. Changes to These Terms

EcoSprout may update these terms and privacy practices when the website, services or business requirements change. Updated information will be published on this page.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about these terms, your personal information, or EcoSprout services, please contact us.
